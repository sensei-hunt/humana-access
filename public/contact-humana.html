Common Reasons to Contact Us

  • Account access — help with sign-in issues, password resets, or account registration
  • Balance inquiries — questions about your HSA, FSA, or HRA balance
  • Claims — help submitting or checking the status of a reimbursement claim
  • Debit card — report a lost or stolen card, activate a new card, or troubleshoot a declined transaction
  • Eligible expenses — find out if a specific item or service qualifies for reimbursement
  • Enrollment — questions about your spending account enrollment or contribution changes

Before You Call

To help us assist you quickly, please have the following information ready:

  • Your member ID or the last four digits of your Social Security number
  • Your date of birth
  • The name of your employer or benefits plan
  • Details about your question or issue (dates, amounts, provider names)
