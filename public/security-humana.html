Protecting Your Spending Account

Your Humana Access account contains sensitive health and financial information. Follow these security best practices to keep your spending accounts safe from unauthorized access and fraud.

Password & Login Security

Create a Strong Password

Use at least 12 characters with a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using personal information like your name, birthday, or member ID. Never reuse passwords from other websites.

Keep Your Credentials Private

Never share your username, password, or member ID with anyone. Humana Access employees will never ask for your password by phone, email, or text message. If someone requests it, it's a scam.

Sign Out After Each Session

Always sign out when you're finished, especially on shared or public computers. Close the browser window after signing out for extra security. Your session will automatically expire after a period of inactivity.

Recognizing Fraud & Phishing

Important: Humana Access will never send you an email asking you to click a link and enter your password or personal information. If you receive such a message, do not click any links — report it to customer support immediately.

Watch for Phishing Emails

Phishing emails try to trick you into revealing personal information. Look for misspelled sender addresses, urgent language demanding immediate action, and links that don't go to humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com. When in doubt, go directly to the website by typing the address in your browser.

Monitor Your Account Activity

Sign in regularly to review your transactions and claims. If you see charges you don't recognize, contact customer support right away. Early detection is key to preventing further unauthorized use.

Debit Card Safety

  • Store your Humana Access debit card in a secure location, just like a credit card
  • Never write your PIN on the card or keep it in the same wallet
  • Report a lost or stolen card immediately by calling customer support
  • Review debit card transactions online regularly for unfamiliar charges
  • Destroy old cards by cutting through the chip and magnetic strip

Device & Browser Security

  • Keep your operating system and browser updated to the latest version
  • Install and maintain antivirus software on your computer
  • Use a secure, private Wi-Fi connection when accessing your account — avoid public Wi-Fi
  • Enable your device's screen lock with a PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition
  • Don't save your password in browsers on shared computers

What to Do If You Suspect Fraud

If you notice unauthorized transactions, receive suspicious communications, or believe your account has been compromised, take these steps immediately:

  • Call customer support to report the issue and lock your account if needed
  • Change your password right away from a secure device
  • Review recent transactions and note any unauthorized charges
  • Monitor your account closely for the next several weeks

Have a security concern? Contact our support team immediately for assistance.
