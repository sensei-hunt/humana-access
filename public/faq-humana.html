Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your Humana Access spending accounts. Can't find what you need? Contact support.

Account Basics

What spending accounts are available through Humana Access?

Humana Access supports four types of spending accounts: Health Savings Accounts (HSA), Healthcare Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA), Health Reimbursement Arrangements (HRA), and Dependent Care FSAs. Each has different rules for contributions, eligible expenses, and fund rollover. Visit our Spending Accounts Guide for details.

How do I register for an online account?

Go to our registration page and enter your member ID, date of birth, and other required information. Once registered, you can sign in to view balances, submit claims, and manage your accounts.

How do I check my spending account balance?

Sign in to your account to view your current balance for each spending account, recent transactions, and pending claims. Balances update in real time after processed transactions.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email. You'll receive a reset link within minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see the email.

Debit Card

How do I use my Humana Access debit card?

Swipe your card at qualified merchants such as pharmacies, doctor's offices, and vision centers. The purchase is deducted directly from your spending account. Some merchants may require you to select "credit" rather than "debit" at checkout.

My debit card was declined. What should I do?

A card may be declined if your account has insufficient funds, the merchant isn't classified as a qualified provider, or the card hasn't been activated. Check your balance online and call customer support if the issue persists.

How do I report a lost or stolen debit card?

Call customer support immediately to deactivate your card and request a replacement. A new card typically arrives within 7-10 business days. You can still submit claims manually while waiting for the replacement.

Claims & Expenses

How do I submit a claim for reimbursement?

Sign in and navigate to the Claims section. Upload your receipt or Explanation of Benefits, enter the expense details, and submit. Most claims are processed within 3-5 business days.

What expenses are eligible?

Eligible expenses include doctor visits, prescriptions, dental and vision care, over-the-counter medications, and medical equipment. See our complete eligible expenses list for details.

What happens to my unused HSA funds?

HSA funds roll over indefinitely — they never expire and the account is yours even if you change employers or health plans. FSA funds follow use-it-or-lose-it rules, though your employer may offer a grace period or carryover of up to $610.
