How to Submit a Claim

  1. Sign in at humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com
  2. Go to Claims from your dashboard
  3. Select your account (HSA, FSA, HRA, or Dependent Care FSA)
  4. Enter expense details – date of service, provider, amount, and expense type
  5. Upload documentation – receipt, EOB, or itemized statement
  6. Submit and track – claims are typically processed within 3-5 business days

Eligible Expenses

Your spending accounts cover hundreds of qualified medical expenses under IRS Section 213(d). Common eligible items include:

  • Medical: Doctor visits, urgent care, ER visits, lab work, physical therapy, mental health counseling
  • Prescription: All prescription medications, insulin
  • Dental: Cleanings, fillings, crowns, orthodontia, dental X-rays
  • Vision: Eye exams, glasses, contacts, LASIK surgery
  • Over-the-Counter: Pain relievers, allergy meds, cold remedies, sunscreen, first aid, menstrual products
  • Equipment: Hearing aids, CPAP, breast pumps, glucose monitors, orthopedic supports

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Humana Access account?

Visit humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com and click Sign In. Enter your username and password. First-time users should register here.

What is humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com?

humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com is the official Humana Access spending account portal for managing your HSA, FSA, HRA, and Dependent Care FSA — including balances, claims, debit cards, and tax documents.

What are the 2026 HSA contribution limits?

$4,300 for individual coverage and $8,550 for family coverage, plus an additional $1,000 catch-up contribution if you're 55 or older.

What are the 2026 FSA contribution limits?

Healthcare FSA: $3,300 per employee. Dependent Care FSA: $5,000 per household ($2,500 if married filing separately).

What is the difference between HSA and FSA?

An HSA requires an HDHP, rolls over indefinitely, can be invested, and is portable. An FSA doesn't require an HDHP, has a use-it-or-lose-it rule (with limited rollover up to $640), and is not portable.

How do I check my balance?

Sign in at humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com and your dashboard shows all account balances. Click any account for detailed transaction history.

What happens to unused FSA funds?

FSA funds are use-it-or-lose-it, but your plan may offer a grace period (2.5 months) or carryover (up to $640). HSA funds roll over indefinitely.

Can I invest my HSA funds?

Yes. Once your balance reaches the investment threshold, you can invest in mutual funds, stocks, and bonds. Earnings grow tax-free when used for qualified expenses.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password" on the Sign In page and enter your email to receive a reset link.

How do I contact customer support?
