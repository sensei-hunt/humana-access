Understanding Your Spending Accounts: HSA, FSA & HRA

Humana Access helps you manage multiple types of tax-advantaged spending accounts. Each account type has different rules, benefits, and eligible expenses. This guide explains the key differences so you can make the most of your benefits.

Health Savings Account (HSA)

An HSA lets you save pre-tax money for qualified medical expenses. Unlike an FSA, unused funds roll over year after year and the account is yours even if you change employers. You must be enrolled in a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) to contribute. For 2026, individual contribution limits are $4,300 and family limits are $8,550, with an additional $1,000 catch-up for those 55 and older.

Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

A Healthcare FSA lets you set aside pre-tax dollars for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses. The 2026 contribution limit is $3,050. FSA funds are generally use-it-or-lose-it, though your employer may offer a grace period or carryover option. Your full annual election is available from day one of the plan year.

Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA)

An HRA is funded entirely by your employer to help cover eligible healthcare costs. You don't contribute to it — your employer sets the amount. Unused funds may roll over depending on your plan design. HRAs can be paired with other accounts like an HSA or FSA.

Dependent Care FSA

A Dependent Care FSA covers child care and elder care expenses for dependents, allowing you to pay for day care, after-school programs, and similar services with pre-tax money. The annual household limit is $5,000 ($2,500 if married filing separately).

Which Account Is Right for You?

  • Want long-term savings? An HSA is the best option — funds never expire and can be invested for growth
  • Need full funds upfront? A Healthcare FSA gives you access to your entire annual election immediately
  • Employer-funded? An HRA is set up and funded by your employer at no cost to you
  • Have child or elder care costs? A Dependent Care FSA can save you hundreds in taxes

Using Your Humana Access Debit Card

Your Humana Access debit card is linked to your spending account and can be used at pharmacies, doctor's offices, and other qualified merchants. Simply swipe your card at the point of sale for eligible expenses. Keep your receipts — your plan administrator may request documentation to verify eligibility.
