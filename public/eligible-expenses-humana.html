Eligible Expenses for Your Spending Account

Your Humana Access spending account — whether HSA, FSA, or HRA — can be used to pay for hundreds of qualified healthcare expenses. Below is a comprehensive guide organized by category, based on IRS Section 213(d) guidelines.

Medical & Doctor Visits

  • Primary care and specialist office visits
  • Urgent care and emergency room copays
  • Annual physicals and preventive screenings
  • Lab work, blood tests, and diagnostic imaging
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation
  • Chiropractic and acupuncture services
  • Mental health counseling and therapy

Prescriptions & Over-the-Counter

  • All prescription medications
  • Insulin (no prescription required)
  • Pain relievers, allergy medications, cold remedies
  • Digestive aids and antacids
  • First aid supplies and bandages
  • Sunscreen (SPF 15+)
  • Menstrual care products
  • Thermometers and blood pressure monitors

Dental Care

  • Cleanings, exams, and X-rays
  • Fillings, crowns, bridges, and root canals
  • Orthodontia (braces, aligners, retainers)
  • Dentures and dental implants

Vision Care

  • Eye exams and vision screenings
  • Prescription eyeglasses and contact lenses
  • Contact lens solution
  • LASIK and corrective eye surgery
  • Reading glasses

Medical Equipment

  • Hearing aids and batteries
  • Breast pumps and lactation supplies
  • Glucose monitors and diabetic supplies
  • CPAP machines and supplies
  • Crutches, walkers, and wheelchairs
  • Orthopedic braces and supports

Items NOT Eligible

  • Cosmetic procedures and teeth whitening
  • Gym memberships (unless prescribed by a doctor)
  • Health insurance premiums (HSA may cover some)
  • General vitamins and supplements (unless prescribed)
  • Personal care items (toothpaste, deodorant)
