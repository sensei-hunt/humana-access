Humana Access – Spending Account Portal

Welcome to the official Humana Access spending account portal. Sign in to manage your Health Savings Account (HSA), Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA), Healthcare FSA, or Dependent Care FSA — all from one secure location.

Use this portal to check your spending account balances, view transactions, submit reimbursement claims, browse eligible expenses, and manage your Humana Access debit card. Whether you need to verify an eligible purchase, download a claim form, or review your account activity, everything is available after you sign in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Humana Access spending account?

Go to humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com and click Sign In. Enter your username and password to access your spending account balances, submit claims, and manage your HSA, HRA, or FSA benefits.

What types of spending accounts does Humana Access offer?

Humana Access offers four types of spending accounts: Health Savings Account (HSA), Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA), Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA), and Dependent Care Flexible Spending Account. Each account has different eligibility requirements and tax advantages.

How do I check my spending account balance?

Sign in to your Humana Access account at humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com to view your current HSA, HRA, or FSA balance, recent transactions, and available funds on your spending account debit card.

What expenses are eligible for my spending account?

Eligible expenses vary by account type but generally include medical, dental, and vision expenses such as copays, prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, and medical equipment. Dependent Care FSA covers childcare and elder care expenses. Check the eligible expenses section for your specific account type.

How do I use my Humana Access debit card?

Use your Humana Access debit card at the point of sale for eligible healthcare purchases. The card automatically draws from your spending account. Keep receipts for documentation. If a purchase requires verification, you may need to submit documentation through the portal.

Is this the official Humana Access website?

Yes — this is the official Humana Access spending account portal. Always confirm you are on humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com before entering your credentials.

Always confirm you are on humanabeta-wealthcareportal.com before entering your username or password.
